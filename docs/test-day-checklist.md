# Test-day checklist

## The day before

- Check the invitation: which version (15 items or 23 items), the deadline, and any instructions about the device or browser.
- Do a short warm-up, not a full test: five or six practice items you have seen before, to remind yourself of the method.
- Sleep. A tired brain miscounts and flips directions, which are the two most common Raven's errors.

## One hour before

- Use a laptop, desktop or tablet with a decent screen, as TalentLens recommends. Not a phone.
- Close other tabs and notifications. Set the room so you will not be interrupted for 45 minutes.
- Have scrap paper and a pen if the instructions allow it, for feature lists on combined items.

## In the first minute

- Read the instructions fully. APM-III starts with four practice items; use them to settle into the format.
- Note the clock. On APM-III, write the checkpoints down: 10 minutes for about 6 items, 20 for about 12, 30 for about 18.

## During the test

- For every item: find the rule on a complete row, predict the answer in words, then match it.
- Name directions in words. Count sides and dots. Do not trust a glance.
- If no rule appears after 20 seconds, run through the families: progression, rotation, distribution, overlay.
- If one item is stuck after two minutes, eliminate what you can, choose, and move on.
- On the adaptive version, harder items mean you are doing well. Do not let them shake you.

## Afterwards

- Write down which rule families felt hard while they are fresh. If there is a supervised re-test later, that list is your study plan.
- Do not try to work out your score from the number you think you got right. The online versions weight harder items more.

## Last practice before the real one

If you have time for one more full run, use the [free 23-puzzle Raven's practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ravens-progressive-matrices?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=7acab2&utm_content=ravens-progressive-matrices) under real timing.
