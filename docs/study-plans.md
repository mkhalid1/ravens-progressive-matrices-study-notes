# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when the invitation has already arrived. Both start with the [15-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know your weak rule families. Expect 30 to 60 minutes a day on the 7-day plan. Matrix practice is tiring in a way reading is not, so shorter daily sessions beat one long one.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the [test overview](test-overview.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Note your two weakest rule families. |
| 2 | Progression and rotation | Read the [progression](sections/progression.md) and [rotation](sections/rotation.md) guides. Do both item sets. For every miss, write one line in a miss log. |
| 3 | Distribution | Read the [distribution guide](sections/distribution.md) and do its items. Then redo yesterday's misses without looking at the answers. |
| 4 | Addition and subtraction | Read the [addition and subtraction guide](sections/overlay.md). Learn the four variants table until you can write it from memory. Do the items. |
| 5 | First timed run | Take a full timed set, such as the [free 23-puzzle test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ravens-progressive-matrices?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ravens-progressive-matrices). Use the 40-minute checkpoints from the [timing guide](timing.md). Sort your misses by rule family. |
| 6 | Combined rules and weak spots | Read the [combined guide](sections/combined.md) and do its items. Then go back to the rule family that cost you most on day 5 and redo its items cold. |
| 7 | Second timed run and rest | Take a second timed set in the morning. Compare with day 5. Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

For each miss, answer three questions in your miss log:

1. **Which rule family was it?** Progression, rotation, distribution, overlay or combined.
2. **What did I get wrong?** Wrong step size, wrong direction, missed a feature, wrong overlay variant, swapped parts.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "count sides, never eyeball" or "test shared elements first".

By day 6 the same two or three mistakes usually repeat. Fixing those is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when the test link has arrived and the deadline is close.

### Day 1: learn the rules

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 20 min | Read the "How the questions look" and "Traps" parts of all five guides. Skip the rest for now. |
| 3 | 40 min | Do the practice items for your two weakest rule families. Read every explanation, including the ones you got right. |
| 4 | 40 min | Take a full timed set, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ravens-progressive-matrices?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ravens-progressive-matrices). |

### Day 2: practise and settle

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 min | Review yesterday's timed misses by rule family. |
| 2 | 30 min | Do the [combined rules](sections/combined.md) items. |
| 3 | 15 min | Read the [timing guide](timing.md) and the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 4 | Rest | Take the real test when you are fresh, not straight after a long practice session. |

## What not to do

- **Do not memorise answers.** APM-III draws items at random from a bank and Raven's Adaptive picks items to match your level, so you will not see the same items. TalentLens notes this protects the test from shared screenshots. Learn rules, not pictures.
- **Do not cram the night before.** Matrix reasoning depends on a fresh, focused mind. A tired brain misreads directions and miscounts.
- **Do not get outside help on an unsupervised test.** TalentLens advises employers to confirm unsupervised results later, for example with a supervised re-test. ([APM-III FAQ](https://www.talentlens.com/content/dam/school/global/Global-Talentlens/fr/documents-solutions/DOCUMENTS-RAVEN/TALENTLENS_TLO_APM_III_FAQ_v022019.pdf))
