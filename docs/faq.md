# FAQ

The first five questions are the ones Google shows most often under "People also ask" for this test (checked 24 November 2026). The rest are questions candidates ask about the job versions.

## Is the Raven test an IQ test?

It is a test of non-verbal reasoning, which researchers treat as a strong measure of general reasoning ability. TalentLens describes it as measuring fluid intelligence and general cognitive ability. In hiring it is used as an ability test and reported as a percentile, not as an IQ number. Clinical versions such as [Raven's 2](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/store/en/usd/p/100001960.html) do give a standard score, which is the kind of score IQ-style tests use.

## What is a Raven evaluation?

It means taking one of the Raven's Progressive Matrices tests: a series of visual patterns, each with a missing piece, where you choose the piece that completes the pattern. Employers use the online versions (Raven's Adaptive, APM-III) to assess candidates; psychologists and schools use paper or clinical versions.

## How do you do Raven's Progressive Matrices?

Look at one complete row, find what changes and how, check it on a second row, then predict the missing piece before you look at the options. Most items use one or more of five rule families: [progression](sections/progression.md), [rotation](sections/rotation.md), [distribution of three](sections/distribution.md), [addition and subtraction](sections/overlay.md) and [combinations](sections/combined.md) of these. Each guide gives a step-by-step method.

## Is SPM an IQ test?

SPM is the Standard Progressive Matrices. Pearson sells Raven's in Coloured, Standard and Advanced versions, and the Standard one is the general-population version. Like the other Raven's tests, it measures non-verbal reasoning and is scored against norm groups. The versions you are likely to meet when applying for a job are the online Raven's Adaptive and APM-III, not the SPM.

## What is a good score on Raven's Progressive Matrices?

There is no published pass mark. Your result is a percentile against a comparison group chosen by the employer, so "good" means good enough for that employer's threshold. See [how scoring works](scoring.md).

## How long is the Raven's test?

Raven's Adaptive has 15 items and takes about 12 minutes; it is untimed. APM-III has 23 items and a 40-minute maximum. The classic paper APM is usually untimed and takes 40 to 60 minutes. ([Raven's Adaptive](https://www.talentlens.com/raven-adaptive.html), [APM-III FAQ](https://www.talentlens.com/content/dam/school/global/Global-Talentlens/fr/documents-solutions/DOCUMENTS-RAVEN/TALENTLENS_TLO_APM_III_FAQ_v022019.pdf), [Pearson APM](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/store/en/usd/p/100000414.html))

## How many answer options are there?

Eight, on both online versions.

## Is Raven's adaptive?

Raven's Adaptive is: a right answer brings a harder item and a wrong one an easier item. APM-III is not adaptive in that sense; it draws 23 items at random from a bank, with forms balanced for difficulty.

## Will I get the same questions as other candidates?

Very unlikely. Both online versions draw from item banks, and TalentLens says this means two candidates do not receive exactly the same set. That is also why memorising answers from the internet does not work.

## Can I take it on my phone?

TalentLens recommends a desktop, laptop or tablet with a reliable connection and a large enough screen for APM-III. Use a proper screen if you can.

## Can practice improve my score?

Practice cannot change your underlying reasoning ability, but it removes the things that cost unprepared candidates points: not knowing the common rule types, misreading directions, miscounting, and poor pacing. Those are exactly what the guides in this repo target.

## Can I retake the test?

Pearson does not publish retake rules. They depend on the employer. Some employers re-test shortlisted candidates under supervision, as the APM-III FAQ recommends.

## Is there an official practice test?

We did not find a free official Raven's practice test on TalentLens's Raven's product page (checked 24 November 2026). APM-III includes four practice items at the start of the real test.

## Where can I practise for free?

- The 10 practice items and the 10-item diagnostic in this repo, each with a full explanation.
- The [free 23-puzzle Raven's practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ravens-progressive-matrices?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=7acab2&utm_content=ravens-progressive-matrices), timed, in the APM-III style.
