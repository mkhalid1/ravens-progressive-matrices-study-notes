# For job applicants

Most people searching for Raven's study help are job candidates who have just received an online assessment link. This page is about that situation.

## Where Raven's sits in a hiring process

TalentLens says Raven's is widely used in graduate recruitment and for roles across many sectors, and that APM-III is aimed at graduate, manager and senior roles. It is usually one of the first online steps: you get an email invitation, take the test at home, and the result is used to decide who moves on to interviews or an assessment centre.

TalentLens also suggests pairing cognitive tests with personality questionnaires, so do not be surprised if Raven's arrives alongside one.

## Unsupervised now, maybe supervised later

Both online versions can be taken unsupervised at home. The APM-III FAQ advises employers to treat unsupervised results with caution and to confirm them later in the process, for example with a supervised re-test or an exercise that measures the same ability. In practice: the score you earn at home may be checked again. Prepare so that you can repeat it.

## Device and setting

TalentLens recommends taking APM-III on a desktop, laptop or tablet with a reliable internet connection and a screen big enough to see the details. Avoid a phone. Small screens make it hard to tell six dots from seven or a hexagon from a heptagon.

## Adjustments

If you have a disability or condition that affects how you take a timed visual test, ask the employer for adjustments before you start. The employer, not the publisher, arranges them, and it is much easier before the test than after.

## Why employers like it

From TalentLens's own description: the questions are diagrammatic, so language and cultural background have less effect on the result, which makes it useful for hiring across countries; it is linked to how quickly people learn and solve new problems; and the adaptive version gives a reliable score in about 12 minutes. ([Raven's Adaptive](https://www.talentlens.com/raven-adaptive.html))
