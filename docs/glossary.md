# Glossary

**Adaptive test.** A test that picks each next item based on your previous answers. Raven's Adaptive gives a harder item after a right answer and an easier one after a wrong answer.

**APM.** Advanced Progressive Matrices, the Raven's version for higher-ability adults and adolescents.

**APM-II.** A fixed 23-item short form of the APM, where every candidate gets the same items.

**APM-III.** The online, item-bank version of the APM: 23 items drawn at random, 40 minutes maximum.

**Combined rules.** An item where two or more rules control different features at once.

**Distractor.** A wrong option designed to look right, usually by getting one feature right and another wrong.

**Distribution of three.** A rule where each row holds the same three values, once each, in a different order.

**Feature.** One property of a cell that can change: shape, count, size, shading, direction, position or inner part.

**Fluid intelligence.** The ability to reason about new problems without relying on learned knowledge. TalentLens describes Raven's as measuring it.

**Item bank.** A large pool of items from which each candidate's test is drawn, so different people get different items.

**Matrix.** The grid of cells in a Raven's item, usually 3 by 3 with the bottom-right cell missing.

**Norm group.** The comparison group your score is measured against, for example managers or graduates.

**Overlay.** A rule where the third cell is made by combining the first two: adding, keeping shared parts, cancelling shared parts or subtracting.

**Percentile.** The share of a norm group you scored the same as or higher than. The 70th percentile means you scored at or above 70% of the group.

**Progression.** A rule where something changes by a fixed step from cell to cell.

**Raven's 2.** The second edition of Raven's Progressive Matrices, for ages 4 to 90, used mainly in clinical and educational settings.

**Rotation.** A rule where a shape turns by a fixed angle from cell to cell.

**SPM.** Standard Progressive Matrices, the general-population Raven's test, between the Coloured (for younger children) and Advanced versions.

**Theta score.** An ability estimate from item response theory, used by the online Raven's versions and converted to a percentile in reports. On APM-III it runs from minus 4 to plus 4.
