# Timing and pacing

## The budget

| Version | Items | Time | Average per item |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raven's APM-III | 23 (after 4 practice items) | 40 minutes maximum | About 1 minute 45 seconds |
| Raven's Adaptive | 15 | Untimed, about 12 minutes typical | About 50 seconds at the typical pace |
| Classic paper APM | Sets 1 and 2 | Untimed, usually 40 to 60 minutes; some administrators time it | Depends on the session |

Sources: [Raven's Adaptive](https://www.talentlens.com/raven-adaptive.html), [APM-III FAQ](https://www.talentlens.com/content/dam/school/global/Global-Talentlens/fr/documents-solutions/DOCUMENTS-RAVEN/TALENTLENS_TLO_APM_III_FAQ_v022019.pdf), [Pearson APM](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/store/en/usd/p/100000414.html).

## A pacing plan for 40 minutes

For APM-III, items are drawn at random from a bank, so easy and hard items are mixed. Use checkpoints rather than a fixed time per item:

| Time used | Aim to have finished |
|---|---|
| 10 minutes | 6 or 7 items |
| 20 minutes | 12 or 13 items |
| 30 minutes | 18 items |
| 38 minutes | All 23, with a guess entered for anything still open |

If you are behind at a checkpoint, give the next hard item a two-minute limit: eliminate what you can, choose, and move on.

## The untimed adaptive version

"Untimed" does not mean "take forever". The adaptive test adjusts to your answers, so careful accuracy is worth more than speed. Take the time to check each answer against two rows, but do not spend five minutes on a single item; if a rule will not appear, choose the option that survives the most checks.

## Where time usually goes

- **Searching for a rule that is not there.** If row 1 shows no progression after 20 seconds, switch to the distribution check, then the overlay check.
- **Comparing options before predicting.** Predicting the answer in words first, then matching, is faster than scanning eight pictures.
- **Re-checking easy items.** One check against row 2 is enough on a clean progression item.
- **Hard combined items.** These deserve more time, but not unlimited time.

## Practise against the clock

Do the practice items in this repo untimed first to learn the rules, then a full timed set to learn the pace. The [free Raven's practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ravens-progressive-matrices?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ravens-progressive-matrices) has 23 puzzles, the same count as APM-III, so you can practise the 40-minute checkpoints.
