# Raven's Progressive Matrices Study Notes

A free, complete study guide for Raven's Progressive Matrices, the non-verbal reasoning test employers use in online assessments: which version you will get, the five kinds of rule behind the puzzles with a clear method for each, 65 original matrix puzzles with full explanations, how the adaptive scoring really works, and study plans for a week or a weekend. Every puzzle is drawn as a picture in the real format: a 3 by 3 grid, one missing cell, eight options. Facts about the test come only from Pearson and TalentLens, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Take the [free timed Raven's practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ravens-progressive-matrices?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ravens-progressive-matrices) (23 puzzles), or the shorter [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md) in this repo.

## Start here

1. **Find your weak rule families.** Take the [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md). It has three puzzles for each kind of rule and tells you where to start.
2. **Fix the weakest one first.** Open its guide below, learn the method and the traps, then do its 10 practice puzzles.
3. **Follow a plan.** Use the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan), or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if your test link has already arrived.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Publisher | Pearson TalentLens |
| Job versions | Raven's Adaptive and Raven's APM-III, both online |
| Raven's Adaptive | 15 items, about 12 minutes, untimed, adaptive |
| Raven's APM-III | 23 items after 4 practice items, 40 minutes maximum, drawn from an item bank |
| Item format | 3 by 3 design with one piece missing, 8 answer options |
| What it measures | Fluid intelligence: abstract, non-verbal reasoning |
| Result | Percentile against a norm group the employer chooses |
| Pass mark | None published. Each employer sets its own. |

Sources: [Raven's Adaptive](https://www.talentlens.com/raven-adaptive.html) and the [APM-III FAQ](https://www.talentlens.com/content/dam/school/global/Global-Talentlens/fr/documents-solutions/DOCUMENTS-RAVEN/TALENTLENS_TLO_APM_III_FAQ_v022019.pdf), checked 24 November 2026.

## The five rule families at a glance

Raven's has no named sections: every item is a matrix. But nearly every item is built from these five kinds of rule.

| Rule family | What changes | Typical trap | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Progression | A count, size, number of sides or shade, step by step | Assuming a step of one | [Progression](docs/sections/progression.md) |
| Rotation and movement | A shape turns, or travels round the cell | Turning the wrong way | [Rotation](docs/sections/rotation.md) |
| Distribution of three | Each row holds the same three things, shuffled | Looking for a sequence that is not there | [Distribution](docs/sections/distribution.md) |
| Addition and subtraction | Cell 3 combines, cancels or subtracts cells 1 and 2 | Assuming plain addition | [Addition and subtraction](docs/sections/overlay.md) |
| Combined rules | Two or three of the above at once | Stopping after the first rule | [Combined rules](docs/sections/combined.md) |

Every guide has the same parts: what it measures, how the questions look, a step-by-step method, the traps that cost points, timing advice, 10 original practice puzzles with explanations, and a score table.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page has links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Each answer is hidden under "Answer and explanation". Commit to a letter before you open it.
- **Predict before you look.** Cover the eight options, work out the missing cell in words, then uncover them. This is the single most useful habit for Raven's.
- **Read every explanation,** including for puzzles you got right. Each one says why the most tempting wrong option is wrong.
- **Keep a miss log.** For each miss, write the rule family and the mistake. The [study plans](docs/study-plans.md#how-to-review-a-missed-item) show how.
- **Practise timed.** The notes teach the rules. A timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ravens-progressive-matrices?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ravens-progressive-matrices), teaches the pace.
- **Clone or fork it** if you want your own copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

## About the practice puzzles

All 65 puzzles (50 in the guides, 15 in the diagnostic) are original, designed for these notes. None are taken from Raven's tests, from Pearson's materials or from any prep site. Each picture is generated from a written specification by [tools/items.py](tools/items.py), which also writes the answer and the explanation, so a picture and its answer cannot drift apart. Every puzzle was also solved independently, without the answer key, before publishing.
